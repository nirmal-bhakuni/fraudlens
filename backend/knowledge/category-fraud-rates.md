# Fraud rate by category

In the training data, the categories with the highest fraud rates were shopping_net (1.76%), misc_net (1.45%) and grocery_pos (1.41%), followed by shopping_pos (0.72%) and gas_transport (0.47%). The other nine categories were between 0.15% and 0.31%, and health_fitness was the lowest at 0.15%. These are rates in simulated data, and the model uses the category as one input among several. A high-rate category does not make a transaction fraud, and a low-rate category does not make it safe.
