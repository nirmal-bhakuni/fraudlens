# Online purchases

Categories ending in _net are online purchases, where the card is not physically present. In the training data, shopping_net and misc_net had the highest fraud rates (about 1.8% and 1.5%), but grocery_net was low (about 0.3%). grocery_pos, an in-store category, was also high (about 1.4%). So "online" alone does not explain the risk. A category is a risk signal, not a verdict.
